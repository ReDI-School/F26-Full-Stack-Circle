Goal of this session: not to make anyone a Scrum expert. Just enough so that everyone understands the words we will use every day (ticket, sprint, board, review) and knows our team rules.

Why do we need this?

15 people, one codebase

💥

Stepping on toes

Two people build the same thing, or change the same file at the same time.

🤷

Nobody knows the status

"Is the login page done?" "Who is working on search?" "Is anyone blocked?"

🐢

Work gets stuck

Someone struggles alone for days and the rest of the team waits.

Real teams solve this with a shared way of working. The most common one is called Agile.

Two ways to build software

Waterfall vs Agile

What is Agile?

Four simple ideas

🧩

Small steps

Split big work into small pieces you can finish in a few days.

✅

Working software

A small feature that works beats a big one that is "90% done".

💬

Talk and get feedback

Show your work early. Ask questions. Review each other's code.

🔁

Adapt

Plans change. Look back regularly and improve how you work.

What is Scrum?

Scrum is a "recipe" for Agile

Agile is the idea. Scrum is one popular way to do it, with three ingredients:

📋

A backlog

The list of everything we want to build, split into tickets.

⏱️

Sprints

Short, fixed periods of work. Ours are 2 weeks.

🗓️

Ceremonies

Regular meetings to plan, show the work, and improve.

Who does what?

Roles in Scrum

🧭

Product Owner

Decides what to build and in which order. Writes and prioritises the tickets.

🛠️

Development Team

Decides how to build it, and builds it. Designers, developers, testers.

🤝

Scrum Master

Helps the team follow the process and removes things that block them.

In our cohort: you are the team and you own the delivery. Teachers guide and answer questions.

The rhythm

One sprint = 2 weeks

Start: plan the sprint.

During: build, open PRs, review each other.

End: show what shipped, then talk about how it went.

Then: the next sprint starts. 🔁

Many companies also do a short daily standup meeting. We don't run one.

Ceremony 1

🗓️ Sprint Planning

"What will we build in the next 2 weeks?"

What happens

  • Look at the top of the backlog together
  • Make sure each ticket is clear — ask questions!
  • Move the tickets for this sprint to Todo

What you bring

  • Your status: what is still open from last sprint?
  • Honesty about your time this sprint
  • Questions about the tickets you might pick

Ceremony 2

🎬 Sprint Review

"Look what we built!"

What happens

  • Each person demos what they shipped — in the running app, not in the code
  • The team gives feedback
  • New ideas or bugs become new tickets

Tip

Every pull request gets a Vercel preview link — a live copy of the app with your changes. Perfect for a demo.

Only finished, merged work counts as "shipped".

Ceremony 3

🔍 Retrospective

"How did we work together — and what should we change?"

😀

What worked

"Pairing on the header saved me a whole day."

😕

What didn't

"My PR waited 4 days for a review."

💡

What we change

"Check Slack for review requests every day."

It is about the process, not about blaming people.

Our working agreement

Our team rules

1
One ticket in progress per personFinish or hand it off before you pick up the next one.
2
7 days max per ticketAfter that it is reassigned. Not a punishment — it keeps the product moving.
3
Open your PR on day oneA draft is fine. Push every day — nothing waits only on your laptop.
4
Stuck? Pair programFind a teammate. Struggling alone for hours helps no one.
5
Blocked by someone's PR? Say it in SlackThey know you are waiting — and someone else can step in.
6
Be clear and immediateNeed a review or help? Post in Slack right away, in public channels, not DMs.
7
Reviews are peer-to-peerAsk teammates. Teachers join occasionally — don't wait for them.
8
AI is allowed — carefullyStill learning? Write the code yourself first, use AI to explain and review.
All from CONTRIBUTING.md. Rule 8: if you are already comfortable developing on your own, AI can help you move faster. If you are still learning the fundamentals, skipping that step now will cost you when you are job-hunting.
